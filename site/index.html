THE FUNDAMENTAL INSIGHT

Is experience.

A failed startup shouldn't reduce someone's employment value. Someone who built a company, managed people, raised capital, negotiated contracts, survived a cash-flow crisis, or made a strategic mistake and recovered — carries capability a résumé line can't communicate.

We treat experience itself as a form of capital.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Build your Experience Graph

Not a résumé — a map of what you built, the scale you operated at, the problems you hit, and what you actually learned from the outcome.

02

The AI investigates, it doesn't collect

A dynamic interview probes your claims, goes deeper on real signal, and tests adjacent capability where experience is thin. No rehearsed Q&A.

03

Employers get evidence, not a stack of résumés

A short list of AI-qualified candidates, each with a Candidate Brief built on demonstrated capability — not keyword overlap.

04

You get hired for what you actually lived through

Your unconventional career, your near-misses, your recoveries — searchable, and valuable, for the first time.
